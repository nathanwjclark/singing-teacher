# Integrated native capture

The main iPhone screen has one Start capture / Stop workflow. Ordinary camera/depth/voice capture remains the default. “Add sound measurement after video” explicitly requests a second, sequential audio-only phase. Advanced sound setup prepares the route silently, exports its level-check request, and imports the reviewed physical-output check; Return to camera restores the preview.

Only a successful natural ten-second video completion may advance into sound. User Stop, backgrounding, interruption, start/export failure, or retry cancels that advancement. The sound route takes exclusive microphone ownership after the video archive saves. Preparation is asynchronous: retained raw check evidence is revalidated against the newly prepared route, volume, placement, protocol and expiry. Missing/mismatched evidence skips sound with a reason. Playback also checks current route and active application state; route/volume/session changes stop it. A calibrated-instrument level check remains required. No playback was exercised during implementation.

Both original private archives remain on the phone. A successful sound save creates a session ZIP containing those original ZIPs unchanged plus session.json with their SHA-256 digests, byte counts, phase order, and partial-stop status. The video and sound manifests share the collection session ID; sound also links the video capture ID. No simultaneity, matched pose, response usability, or inclusion in a fit is asserted. Existing importers still consume the individual original archives; unpack the session wrapper first. A failed combined export preserves both originals.

The main camera view now includes a separate raw depth thumbnail at up to 10 Hz, including before recording. Native depth is displayed unmirrored, rotated for portrait viewing, and without interpolation. Black means no finite positive depth return; valid values use a fixed grayscale window. This is neither confidence nor calibrated accuracy. The thumbnail explicitly says paused when the camera is paused, and clears when a synchronized depth sample is missing. Move farther away if the visible tongue is black while deeper mouth pixels have returns.

Validation: unsigned iOS device build; a focused executed Swift linked-export check preserved binary fixture bytes and verified ZIP contents/hashes, sequential metadata, and an interrupted-sound status. Physical playback and measured SPL validation remain outstanding.
