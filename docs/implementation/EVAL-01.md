# EVAL-01: independent prospective evaluator

`src/evaluation/index.ts` exports `evaluatePrediction(input): Promise<EvaluationRecord>` and the `EvaluationInput`, `BaselineInput`, `HeldOutSet` types. The evaluator receives scoring truth only after commitment and acquisition. It contains no fitting, inference-engine, or update operation. Callers should keep held-out audio and split manifests out of learner/planner inputs.

Required inputs are KIT `PredictionCommit`, `ObservationBundle`, and one preregistered `AudioMeasurement` window, the actual capture-start time, held-out observation IDs, solver calls used and articulation parameter count. Optional held-out participant/session lists enforce those split levels; same-person personalized future-trial evaluation can use observation-level separation. Include fitting artifact hashes to detect renamed media. Optional baseline includes its own prior commit, name, solver usage and articulation count.

`absolute-error-v1` reports |prediction − observation| for each exact name/unit match in native units. No normalization or combined score conceals different scales. Forecast uncertainty remains in the immutable commit; this rule does not claim to score calibration of predictive distributions. Unknown rules, altered digests, target-evidence overlap, unregistered held-out targets, invalid chronology/clock/artifact references and unfair baseline budgets fail explicitly. Missing preregistered features exclude the whole comparison, preserving the reason rather than cherry-picking successful dimensions. Baselines must have identical targets, intervention, evaluation mode, declared solver budget and articulation freedom; actual calls are recorded and must fit the allowance.

No engine or no numerical forecast means the orchestration job remains blocked/unavailable before commitment (KIT disallows committing unavailable forecasts). No acquired audio means no score. Development-fixture observations are excluded from scientific synthetic evaluation. This module does not produce benchmark trials on its own. With no baseline, it may report absolute errors but explicitly marks comparative improvement unavailable.

The JSON snapshot plus SHA-256 catches accidental edits, not a malicious producer that rewrites every record and digest. Locally supplied capture timestamps, split declarations, source hashes and solver counters must come from trusted capture/ledger producers. External dataset replay dates must not masquerade as prospective human acquisitions. Stronger adversarial custody, hidden engine-case generation and anatomy/geometry recovery scores await a genuine engine and a scoring-only reference store. Never infer anatomical accuracy or singing quality from an acoustic error alone.

Light validation: `node --experimental-strip-types --test src/evaluation/evaluation.test.ts` and `npx tsc -b`. The fixture checks numerical error, altered commitments, chronology, renamed-media leakage, unfair baseline freedom and unavailable features; no datasets are downloaded by tests.
