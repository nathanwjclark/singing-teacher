## Scientific rules

Separate anatomy, source, articulation, acquisition response, cue execution and subjective sensations. A better microphone fit may come from nuisance flexibility; it does not establish better anatomy. Declared controls are not measured execution. Report the retained alternatives and out-of-support evidence, and do not call heuristic weights or objective-gap sets calibrated probabilities.

Freeze predictions and versions before their target observation. Match learning histories by exact geometry/native/extractor/profile/cue/context identities, retaining all parent model IDs; a changed model ID alone is not evidence that anatomy changed. Do not use outcomes to choose their own candidate bank. Preserve stopped, missing and unsuccessful attempts.

Keep native synthesis budgets explicit and bounded. Independent evaluation owns its held-out data and does not share target observations for tuning. Evaluate unseen vowel/pitch/source and capture perturbations, as well as missing and ambiguous evidence. Software correctness and scientific accuracy have separate outcomes.
