# Articulatory data shortlist

Checked 2026-09-10 against creator/institutional publications and repositories. No corpus was downloaded or trained on. These resources can support bounded articulatory/acoustic experiments; none provides a turnkey set of internal muscle positions paired with expert ratings of good singing. That conclusion concerns this shortlist, not proof that no such dataset exists.

| Dataset | Measured evidence | Access and dataset license | Practical use and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| USC-TIMIT | Synchronized rtMRI + audio for 10 speakers; EMA + audio for four. MRI and EMA are separate sessions of the same sentences. | [Creator page](https://sail.usc.edu/span/usc-timit/), [8.5 GB release](https://zenodo.org/records/19422914). CC BY 4.0 in the [repository metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/19422914). | A small tongue/jaw motion versus acoustic-feature pilot. Never treat separate-session MRI/EMA as simultaneous ground truth. Speech, not singing-quality labels. |
| USC 75-Speaker Speech MRI Database | Sagittal dynamic MRI with synchronized audio, sustained-sound 3D vocal-tract MRI and static upper-airway anatomy. | [Creator page](https://sail.usc.edu/span/75speakers/), [versioned data release](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.13725546.v1), [license metadata](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/13725546). CC BY 4.0. | Best candidate here for speaker-held-out morphology/articulation work. Tissue boundaries and airspace are observable; activation of named muscles is not. Scanner conditions differ from webcam singing. Start with a few consent-compatible subjects, not the whole corpus. |
| MOCHA-TIMIT | Audio, EMA points on tongue/lips/incisors/velum, laryngograph and electropalatography. Front-mouth video is by special request. | [University instrumentation and access page](https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/research/projects/artic/mocha.html), [data directory](https://data.cstr.ed.ac.uk/mocha/). Creator states free for noncommercial use; not an unrestricted open-data license. | Useful compact articulator trajectories, contact and acoustic checks. EMA measures sensor coordinates; EPG measures palate contact; neither gives individual internal muscle activation. Verify release contents, permissions and channels before importing. |
| Silent Speech EMG (Gaddy & Klein) | Surface facial electrical signals during voiced/silent speech, raw/cleaned audio and text. Eight EMG channels; single-speaker corpus in the paper. | [Creator repository](https://github.com/dgaddy/silent_speech), [3.9 GB dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/4064409), CC BY 4.0 per [dataset metadata](https://zenodo.org/api/records/4064409); [paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.445/). | Actual electrical evidence is closer to muscle activity than image landmarks, but gives neither internal 3D geometry nor good/bad singing labels. Voiced audio is synchronized with voiced EMG; silent targets are transferred from separate voiced renditions. One speaker cannot establish cross-person performance. |

The public MNGU0 domain currently resolves to unrelated general speech content; do not use that domain as an authoritative download or license source. It was excluded from the shortlist pending a verified institutional archive.

## A bounded first experiment

Choose one permitted corpus and version, freeze a tiny manifest of speaker/session/utterance IDs and media hashes, and reserve whole speakers or whole sessions before selecting models. Keep adjacent frames and re-encoded versions of the same recording in one split. For personalized same-singer trials, reserve future recordings and their acoustic targets. Preserve actual scanner/microphone preprocessing in provenance. Extract the same AUD-01 feature definitions from recorded and synthesized audio, without tuning on held-out outputs.

Score a prior numeric forecast with EVAL-01. Compare fixed generic anatomy versus individualized anatomy with the same articulation freedom and solver allowance. Report every missing feature, failed attempt and excluded case. Labeled reference geometry should be scored separately after coordinate registration; acoustic agreement alone cannot identify hidden muscles. MRI/EMA trajectories could validate tongue/jaw motion, while separately collected teacher ratings would be needed to evaluate coaching quality. Do not train on a corpus and then claim recovery from held-out clips of the same underlying capture.

No dataset in this shortlist validates inference of internal tongue muscles from a phone photo or the current color-based tongue tracker. Initial bootstrap images can map visible surfaces and store user-specific evidence, with unavailable internal anatomy kept explicit.
