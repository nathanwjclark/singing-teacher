## Actual remaining evidence requirements

Full Xcode/iOS SDK build and device interruption/camera-switching checks require
an appropriate Apple development environment. Swift parsing alone does not
satisfy that requirement. Physical rear LiDAR/TrueDepth coverage, calibration,
pixel mapping and reference-target error require actual device measurements.

and instructional comprehension/learning effects require their own evidence.

negative outcomes; they do not establish internal anatomy, tissue mechanics,
vocal-fold contact or coaching efficacy.

These requirements do not make all software work blocked on B. A continues the
owned visual and teaching integrations and remaining concrete regression fixes.

manual backend processing steps the learner must perform in the app.
