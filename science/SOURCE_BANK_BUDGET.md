# App source-bank budget

The optional source fit preserves the same native source support for every retained anatomy along one declared shape axis. The default geometric glottis uses PS values `[-0.2, 0, 0.2]` for one or two geometries and `[-0.2, 0.2]` for three through eight. With `PHONATION_SOURCE_MODEL=two_mass`, the two-mass model uses rest displacement `XB=XT` of `[.005, .01, .015]` cm or `[.005, .015]` cm with `EAA=0 cm²` and `DF=1` fixed. Both families prescribe `JA=-3`, `PR=8000 dPa` and gain 2. More than eight geometries are explicitly unsupported. Geometry count never silently collapses source support to one fixed value. See [native two-mass source family](MECHANICAL_SOURCE.md).

The normal five-geometry profile therefore fits ten source/tract candidates. Its joint, fixed-source and fixed-anatomy comparisons require 30 native synthesis calls; the corresponding prospective bank freezes all 30 alternatives with another 30 calls. The maximum eight-geometry profile has 16 candidates and 48 alternatives. Each app phase retains its 90-second numerical deadline; missing or timed-out alternatives remain explicit, and incomplete source fitting does not replace a valid prior model.

These are bounded simulator alternatives, not inferred vocal-fold contact or calibrated anatomical probabilities. Held-out bank scoring extracts the original observation once, performs no new synthesis, preserves the baseline anatomy model, and retains conditional ranking provenance separately.

`test_source_app_budget.py` checks equal one-axis source support for both families and the numerical call bounds. `test_app_source_loop.py` verifies actual fit/bank call counts, two later recordings, ranking parents and replay through the app and native worker, and runs fit, bank and score once in two-mass mode; its runtime depends on available compute.
