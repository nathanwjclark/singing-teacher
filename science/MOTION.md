# MOT-02 measured visible trajectories

`observations.geometry.motion` reconstructs calibrated visible landmark trajectories and expresses them in a separately measured rigid head coordinate frame. It reuses `DepthFrame`, depth masking/covariance and the source-defined lip distance bridge. It does not perform optical tracking, estimate head pose from moving mouth points or complete occluded surfaces.

## Executable boundary

Each `MotionFrame` carries a unique frame ID, a genuine `DepthFrame`, a measured `world_from_head` transform at that depth timestamp, head-pose evidence ID, declared rigid reference IDs, and landmark annotations `{name: {pixel_uv, correspondence_id}}`. Reference IDs must be supplied and cannot be the tracked moving landmark names. The caller remains responsible for proving the reference geometry is rigid and does not move with the gesture. Both camera and head transforms use the same world coordinate system in meters. The numerical head-local position is `R_head.T @ (point_world - translation_head)`; covariance is rotated with the same transform.

`analyze_motion` accepts cue/version/context/attempt identifiers, explicit calibration/held-out split, expected landmark correspondence, and attempt outcome (`completed`, `unsuccessful`, `unknown`). It preserves all captured frames and mapped timestamps, including missing-depth frames and unsuccessful attempts. Duplicate frame IDs, repeated depth evidence, mixed session clocks and nonmonotonic timestamps are rejected. This prevents repeated stale frames from being relabeled as new evidence. Malformed calibration, pixels or head transforms raise errors; missing depth/pose/landmarks or inconsistent correspondence retain an explicit per-landmark reason and produce no coordinate for that frame.

Valid per-frame outputs carry head-local position in meters, conditional covariance in square meters, original depth pixel, evidence and head-pose evidence IDs. These covariance matrices describe depth/pixel tracking noise **conditional on exact calibration and head pose**. Head-pose uncertainty is separately labeled `not_quantified`; the output does not assert total motion uncertainty or an anatomical accuracy. Synchronization uncertainty remains an absolute session-clock bound inherited from reconstruction. The adapter must supply a head pose aligned to that same measurement instant.

No interpolation is performed. Missing observations and gaps longer than the configured `max_gap_seconds` split trajectories. Each continuous segment can report its maximum pairwise **observed excursion**, while the attempt-level excursion is `None` if any expected sample is missing/inconsistent or any gap exists. A single sample cannot establish excursion. Segment extrema are observed samples, not physiological limits or inferred occluded movement. No palate/tongue scan completion or breathing inference is performed.

`repeat_variability` compares observed excursions only within an identical cue/version/context. It retains unsuccessful-attempt IDs and excludes incomplete/gapped attempts from the scalar dispersion calculation. Its sample SD is variability of the observed excursions, not sensor-corrected motor variance, precision of anatomical limits or evidence that an unsuccessful attempt is physically impossible.

## Dynamic inverse bridge

If callers configure expected `upper_lip` and `lower_lip` correspondence plus the declared native outer-lip protocol and positive model-discrepancy sigma, every valid frame carries `geometry_observation` generated by `joint_lip_measurement`. Its `trial_id` is the frame ID. Missing endpoints, head pose or valid depth never produce a geometry measurement. `geometry_status` is separate from overall landmark `visibility`; `geometry_reason` explains every omitted measurement, including an unconfigured lip operator.

Output attempts contain `attempt_id`, `cue_id`, `cue_version`, `context_id`, `context`, `split` and `frames`, matching the dynamic inverse adapter agreed with its owner. B's acoustic extraction supplies `pose`, `frequency_hz` and `magnitude_db` joined by frame ID and verifies synchronization; this module does not invent acoustic samples. The dynamic inverse lane owns the executable multimodal consumption test.
