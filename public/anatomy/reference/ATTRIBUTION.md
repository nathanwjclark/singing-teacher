# Head and neck reference plate

**Head sagittal mouth**, Patrick J. Lynch, medical illustrator; C. Carl Jaffe, MD, cardiologist. Yale University Center for Advanced Instructional Media. Published December 23, 2006.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Head_sagittal_mouth.jpg
Original download: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/13/Head_sagittal_mouth.jpg
License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 Generic (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/).

`lynch-head-sagittal.jpg` is redistributed unchanged. The app displays an animated adaptation: runtime silhouette mask, horizontal reflection, and a deformable mesh driven by shared head pitch, jaw opening, and tongue state. Original anatomical proportions and texture are retained in the resting pose. These illustrative deformations are our additions; the original authors do not endorse the app. The plate is reference anatomy, not a reconstruction of the user or a measurement of internal tissue.
